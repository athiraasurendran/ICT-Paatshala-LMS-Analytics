# Business Questions Explored

The following are analytical questions considered during the project. They are **not** published company findings.

## Learner engagement and learning activity

- How did enrolment vary by month and year?
- How did dropout patterns vary by month and year?
- How did course completion vary by month and year?
- What patterns could be explored in learner activity and assignment submissions?
- How did selected enrolment and completion indicators move in relation to one another?

## Programme performance

- How did programmes compare across enrolment indicators?
- How did programmes compare across completion indicators?
- How did programmes compare across dropout indicators?
- Were there changes in programme-level patterns over time?

## Historical context

- What changes were visible around the COVID-19 period?
- Did patterns differ across time periods before, during, and after that period?

## Interpretation principles

- A higher count is not necessarily a higher rate; use suitable denominators when interpreting rates.
- Comparisons should use consistent metric definitions and time windows.
- Correlation or co-movement does not prove causation.
- Findings require context and validation before operational decisions.
- Actual answers and numeric results are not published because the source data is confidential.
